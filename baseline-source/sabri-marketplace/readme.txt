Sabri Marketplace is a direct buyer-seller marketplace. It provides listings, seller verification, buyer and seller phone/WhatsApp profiles, product-linked internal chat, text, private attachments, voice notes, offers, delivery/pickup discussion, contact sharing, notifications, blocking, reporting and administrative moderation.

The platform does not receive or hold transaction funds. Buyers and sellers independently agree price, payment, inspection, pickup and delivery.
